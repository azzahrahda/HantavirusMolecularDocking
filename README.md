In Silico Molecular Docking Analysis of Curcumin, Quercetin, and Kaempferol Against Hantavirus Nucleocapsid Protein (PDB ID: 5E04)
This Project explores the potential antiviral activity of natural compounds such as Curcumin, Quercetin, and Kaempferol against the Hantavirus nucleocapsid protein (PDB ID: 5E04) using _molecular docking_ analysis. The docking simulation was performed using PyRx with Autodock Vina to predict ligand-protein interactions and evaluate binding affinity as an initial step in antiviral drug discovery

Key Results
| Ligand     | Binding Affinity (kcal/mol) |
| Ribavirin  | -6.3                        |
| Curcumin   | -6.8                        |
| Quercetin  | -8.2                        |
| Kaempferol | -8.2                        |
Quercetin showed the strongest and most diverse interactions with the target protein, indicating its potential as the best inhibitor candidate among the tested compounds.
🧪Software & Tools: PyRx, AutoDock Vina, UCSF Chimera, PyMOL, BIOVIA Discovery Studio Visualizer, Open Babel
🧬Target Protein: Hantavirus Nucleocapsid Protein (PDB ID: 5E04). The nucleocapsid protein plays an important role in viral RNA binding and replication, making it a promising antiviral drug target.
🌿Tested Compounds: Curcumin, Quercetin, Kaempferol, Ribavirin (positive control)
🖼️Molecular Docking Visualization
Protein–ligand interaction visualization was analyzed using BIOVIA Discovery Studio Visualizer and PyMOL
